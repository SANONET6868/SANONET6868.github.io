SANO NET PRO Backup

A private backup utility for the owner of SANO NET PRO, a hotspot and ISP management application.

Purpose

The utility creates encrypted recovery backups of the owner's application, databases, configuration and voucher PDFs, and transfers encrypted backup files to the owner's Google Drive.

Google Drive access

Google authorization is used to upload and retrieve backup files created by this utility. The configured permission is limited to files created or explicitly authorized for the application.

This public website provides information only. It does not provide access to private backups.
